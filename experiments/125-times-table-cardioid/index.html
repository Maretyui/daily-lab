125 — Times Table Cardioid

N points sit evenly around a circle. Point i draws a chord to point (i × k) mod N for every i. Integer k gives a star; letting k drift through real numbers unfolds it into rolling cardioid and epicycloid curves.

Chord endpoints are computed from a continuous angle ((i / N) × 2π), not by indexing into an array, so a fractional k still lands every chord at a real point on the circle instead of snapping to the nearest one. Both sliders are native <input type="range"> elements, so dragging them works the same with a mouse or a finger.
