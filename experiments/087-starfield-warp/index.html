087 — Starfield Warp

A field of stars streams past a fixed vanishing point, each one moving outward at a speed proportional to its own distance from center so closer stars appear to fly by faster — the classic parallax trick behind every warp-speed effect. Drag (or press) anywhere in the stage to push the throttle; release to drift back down to cruising speed.
