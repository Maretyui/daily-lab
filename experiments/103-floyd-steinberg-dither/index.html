103 — Floyd–Steinberg Dither

Pick a photo and it's reduced to two colors with Floyd–Steinberg error-diffusion dithering, run entirely on the pixel data in a <canvas> — no server, no library.

Threshold 128
Each pixel is rounded to pure black or white, and the rounding error is spread to its still-unprocessed neighbors (7/16 right, 3/16 below-left, 5/16 below, 1/16 below-right) instead of being discarded — the classic 1976 algorithm. The two output colors are swapped in afterwards, so you can preview any duotone.
