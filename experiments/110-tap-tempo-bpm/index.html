110 — Tap Tempo

Tap along to a beat (or click/press the pad) and the BPM is derived live from the gaps between taps, not from a fixed metronome. A run resets itself automatically after 2 seconds of silence.

Each tap's timestamp comes from performance.now(). The BPM shown is 60000 / average-gap-of-the-last-8-taps, so one slightly early or late tap gets smoothed out instead of swinging the reading wildly. A tap gap over 2000ms is treated as "stopped", not as a very slow tempo, and the run clears itself.
