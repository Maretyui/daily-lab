099 — Reaction-Diffusion

A Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion simulation running on a coarse grid, stepped every frame and painted to canvas. Drag or tap across the grid to inject the reagent that seeds new growth — the pattern that spreads afterwards comes entirely from the diffusion math, not from anything drawn by the pointer itself.

Two virtual chemicals A and B diffuse across a 128×128 Float32Array pair, react each step with feed/kill rates, and get remapped to pixel brightness via ImageData — the same class of simulation used for organic mottled patterns, unrelated to the particle systems and cellular automata already in this lab.
