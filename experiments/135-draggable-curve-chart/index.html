135 — Draggable Curve Chart

Drag any of the seven control points up or down and the whole curve reflows live through a Catmull-Rom spline — no chart library, just bezier math on a canvas.

Each point only ever moves vertically along its fixed x slot. The curve between them is a Catmull-Rom spline converted to cubic Bezier segments on the fly: for interior point p1 heading to p2, the two Bezier control handles are derived from the previous and next points (p1 + (p2 - p0) / 6 and p2 - (p3 - p1) / 6), so the curve always passes exactly through every point while staying smooth across the join — no manual handle-dragging required.
