Flow field particles

A grid of invisible angles — a "flow field" generated from stacked sine waves rather than a random walk — steers a few hundred short-lived particles. Each frame only paints a translucent black rectangle over the last one instead of clearing it, which is what turns individual particle steps into continuous flowing trails. Press and drag (mouse or finger) to bend the field around your pointer with a temporary vortex.

No external noise library — the field angle at each point comes from three overlapping sin()/cos() waves at different frequencies, which is enough to look organic without ever repeating exactly.
