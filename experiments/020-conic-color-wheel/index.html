Conic gradient color wheel

No swatches, no pre-rendered wheel image - the hue ring is a single conic-gradient() with a mask punched out of its center, and the saturation/value square underneath is two more gradients stacked on top of each other. Dragging just computes an angle and a position; the browser paints every possible color already.

#000000

Drag around the ring to change hue, or anywhere in the square to change saturation and value - both handles also respond to arrow keys once focused. The hex readout, swatch, and square's own gradient all recompute from the same three numbers (hue, sat, val); the ring and square graphics never change, only where the two dots sit on top of them.
