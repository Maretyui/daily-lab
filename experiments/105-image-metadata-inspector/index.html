105 — Image Metadata Inspector

Pick a JPEG or PNG and its header bytes are parsed by hand with a DataView — JPEG's EXIF tags, PNG's chunk list — no library, and the pixels themselves are never decoded.

Metadata

JPEG stores EXIF inside an APP1 segment as a little TIFF structure — a byte-order marker, then an IFD of 12-byte entries pointing at tag, type and either an inline value or an offset into the rest of the segment. PNG instead just chains labelled, length-prefixed chunks one after another from byte 8 onward; IHDR always comes first and gives the real pixel dimensions straight from the file, with no decode needed.
