131 — Metaball Blobs

A handful of drifting blobs that melt into each other on approach instead of overlapping like flat circles. Tap or click anywhere on the canvas to drop in another one.

Each blob contributes radius² / distance² to every point of a low-resolution scalar field (a classic metaball falloff); the summed field is painted into a 150×90 off-screen canvas and scaled up with the browser's own bilinear interpolation, which is what turns a blocky grid of numbers into a smooth, melting edge for free. The threshold slider moves the cutoff value at which a point counts as "inside" a blob.
