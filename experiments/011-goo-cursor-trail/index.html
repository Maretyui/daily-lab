Goo cursor trail

Move the cursor over the panel below. A chain of eight dots trails behind it, each one easing toward the previous dot's last position — and an SVG feGaussianBlur + feColorMatrix filter fuses overlapping dots into a single liquid blob instead of letting them visibly overlap as separate circles.

move your cursor here

The trick is entirely in the filter, not the JS: each dot is a plain blurred circle. feGaussianBlur spreads its edges into a soft gradient, so once two blurred circles get close enough their faint edges physically overlap in the alpha channel. feColorMatrix then multiplies alpha by a steep factor (22) and subtracts an offset (-9), which clips anything below a threshold back to fully transparent and anything above it back to fully opaque — collapsing the soft blur into a hard edge again, but now following the merged silhouette instead of each circle's original round outline.

The trail itself is just requestAnimationFrame lerping: dot 0 chases the real cursor position, dot 1 chases dot 0's last frame, dot 2 chases dot 1's, and so on — no physics library, no external dependency, just eight numbers converging toward each other every frame.
