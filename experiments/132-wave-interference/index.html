132 — Wave Interference

Two or more point sources, each radiating circular waves. Where crests line up they reinforce into bright bands; where a crest meets a trough they cancel into dark ones. Drag a source to move it, or tap empty water to drop in another.

Each source contributes sin(2π × (distance / wavelength − t)) to every point of a low-resolution buffer; the amplitudes are summed and mapped to a blue–white–red scale (trough–node–crest), the same way two speakers playing the same tone create loud and quiet spots around a room.
