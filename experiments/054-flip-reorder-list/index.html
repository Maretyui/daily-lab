Plain HTML5 drag-and-drop for the actual reordering, plus the FLIP technique (First, Last, Invert, Play) for the animation: before the DOM reorders, every item's current position is recorded; right after it reorders, each item is snapped back to its old spot with a negative transform, then eased to zero — so it visually glides to its new slot instead of teleporting.
