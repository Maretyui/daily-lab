077 — Anchor Positioning Tooltip

Every tooltip below is glued to its own button purely through CSS anchor positioning — anchor-name on the trigger, position-anchor/position-area on the popup, and position-try-fallbacks to flip the tooltip to the other side when it would otherwise clip past the viewport edge. No getBoundingClientRect(), no resize listener, no JS positioning math at all.

Anchor positioning currently ships in Chromium (125+); browsers without it just never move the tooltip off its default fixed position and it stays invisible at opacity 0 — a safe, unremarkable fallback rather than a broken layout.
