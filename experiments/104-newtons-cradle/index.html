104 — Newton's Cradle

Drag either end ball outward and let go. Velocity carries through the resting chain one collision at a time, exactly like the desk toy — no physics library, just small-angle pendulum motion and a swap of velocity on contact.

Swings: 0 Transfers: 0
Each ball's horizontal offset follows simple harmonic motion (a = -(g / L) · s) instead of a full nonlinear pendulum, which keeps the per-frame math to one multiply per ball. A collision just swaps two adjacent balls' velocities and nudges them apart — done a few passes a frame so a hit at one end finishes propagating all the way to the other before the next frame draws.
