133 — Boids Flocking

A classic flocking simulation — no bird knows about the flock as a whole, each one only reacts to its nearest neighbors with three rules: don't collide, match their heading, drift toward their center. Tap the canvas to drop in another bird.

Every frame, each bird looks only at neighbors inside its own perception radius and blends three steering vectors: separation (push away from anyone too close), alignment (match the average heading of the group), and cohesion (steer toward the group's average position). The whole-flock movement that emerges is not directed by anything — it falls out of those three local rules alone.
