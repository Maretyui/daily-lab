106 — Mic Pitch Tuner

Tap start, allow microphone access, then hum or sing a steady note. The fundamental frequency is pulled out of the raw waveform with autocorrelation and matched to the nearest musical note.

—
 
 
Autocorrelation shifts a windowed copy of the time-domain buffer against itself and looks for the lag with the strongest correlation — that lag is the waveform's period, so sampleRate / lag gives the frequency directly, no FFT needed. The nearest note comes from 69 + 12 · log2(f / 440) rounded to the closest MIDI number, and the needle shows how many cents sharp or flat the actual pitch is from that note's exact frequency.
