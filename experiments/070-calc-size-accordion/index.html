070 — calc-size() Accordion

An accordion that animates open/closed to a genuinely auto height, no JS scrollHeight measuring and no fixed max-height guess. height: calc-size(auto, size) plus interpolate-size: allow-keywords lets the browser itself resolve and transition to the content's real size.

Your browser doesn't support calc-size()/interpolate-size yet — the panels below will snap open/closed instead of animating.
