Web Audio step sequencer

A four-track 16-step drum machine with no audio files at all - every kick, snare, hi-hat and clap is synthesized on the fly from raw oscillators and filtered noise through the Web Audio API. Timing doesn't rely on setInterval directly triggering sound (which drifts under load); instead a lookahead scheduler wakes up every 25ms, peeks at the audio clock (audioContext.currentTime), and schedules any notes due in the next 100ms precisely, the same technique real browser sequencers use.

BPM 112

Click any cell to toggle it on the current pattern. Kick = pitch-dropping sine burst, snare/clap = bursts of filtered white noise, hi-hat = a short highpassed noise tick - four completely different synthesis recipes sharing one AudioContext.
