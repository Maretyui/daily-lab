138 — String Art Generator

Pegs are placed evenly around a circle and numbered. A single thread jumps from peg to peg in a fixed stride — peg 0 to peg k, then k to 2k, wrapping around — and every straight chord it draws overlaps the others at a different angle. Drag the stride slider to see completely different patterns emerge from the same pegboard, or tap/drag on the canvas to nudge the stride live.

This is the same construction as the classic circular-nail-and-thread craft: pegs points sit evenly spaced on a circle, and the thread visits peg (i * stride) mod pegs for i = 0, 1, 2, … until it returns to peg 0. Because stride and pegs are usually coprime, that walk touches every peg exactly once before closing the loop, and the chords it draws along the way are what make the moiré-like woven look — no pixels are touched directly, only straight lines between two points on the circle, over and over with a different offset each time.
