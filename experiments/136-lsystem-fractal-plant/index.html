136 — L-System Fractal Plant

A tiny string-rewriting grammar — "X" keeps replacing itself with a longer pattern of forward-steps, branches and turns — read character by character by a turtle that only knows how to walk forward, turn, and push/pop its position. Tap the canvas or the button to regrow it with fresh random jitter.

The grammar: axiom X, rules X → F+[[X]-X]-F[-FX]+X and F → FF. Rewriting the string iterations times and then walking it once produces the whole plant in one pass — F steps the turtle forward drawing a line, +/- turn it by the angle (each with a little random jitter, so no two branches are quite identical), and [/] save and restore its position/heading so a branch can rejoin the stem it grew from. X itself never draws — it only exists so the rules have somewhere left to keep expanding.
