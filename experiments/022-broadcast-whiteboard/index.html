Cross-tab whiteboard

A shared drawing surface with no server, no websocket, and no polling. Every stroke segment and every pointer position gets posted through a BroadcastChannel - a same-origin message bus the browser itself provides between tabs, windows and iframes. Open this exact page in a second tab side by side and draw: the strokes and the live cursor appear there instantly, and vice versa.

Solo - open another tab of this page to connect

Only three message shapes cross the channel: a stroke segment (from-point, to-point, color, width), a cursor position sent on every pointermove, and a clear. Each tab tags its messages with a random peerId generated once per tab and ignores its own echoes. A tab that goes quiet for a couple seconds - closed, backgrounded, whatever - simply ages out of the peer list; there's no handshake or disconnect event to listen for, just a timeout.
