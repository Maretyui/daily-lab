124 — Plinko Board

Tap or click anywhere on the board to drop a ball from the top. At every peg it bounces left or right with equal odds, and the bin it lands in builds up a live histogram underneath — drop enough balls and the bars settle into a binomial curve.

0 balls dropped.
Each ball's path is just a coin flip per row — left or right at every peg — rather than a full physics simulation, which is what makes the bin counts converge on a binomial distribution instead of a flat spread. Dropping is driven by pointerdown, so a tap on a phone does the same thing a click does.
