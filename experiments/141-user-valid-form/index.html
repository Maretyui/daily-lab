141 — :user-valid Form Feedback

Same untouched form, same required fields, two CSS rules. Switch the mode below and watch the red borders appear at a completely different moment, with zero JavaScript involved in the decision.

Required, must look like an email
Required, at least 4 characters
Required, 13–120
In :invalid mode every required field lights up red before you've clicked anything at all, just because the page loaded with them empty. Switching to :user-invalid keeps them neutral until you actually type into one and leave it wrong — the browser tracks "has this field been interacted with" entirely on its own, no input/blur listeners written here.
