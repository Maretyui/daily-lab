139 — Mandelbrot Explorer

Tap anywhere to zoom in on that point, drag to pan, and the colors keep drifting on their own between redraws. Zooming far enough keeps turning up more of the same spiral and seahorse shapes at every scale.

Every pixel of a low-resolution off-screen buffer iterates z = z² + c starting from z = 0, where c is that pixel's position on the complex plane — the iteration count before |z| escapes past 2 (smoothed with a log-of-log correction) becomes a hue. Points that never escape within the iteration budget are the Mandelbrot set itself, left black. Only that escape-count buffer is recomputed on tap/drag/zoom; the animation loop just cycles the hue offset over the cached counts every frame, which is why the colors keep moving without re-iterating anything. Iteration budget scales with zoom depth so deeper views stay detailed instead of muddying into flat bands.
