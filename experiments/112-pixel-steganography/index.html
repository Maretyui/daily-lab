112 — Pixel Steganography

Hides a text message inside a photo by rewriting the least significant bit of each red, green and blue channel — a change too small to see, but enough to carry real data back out again.

Extracted message will appear below.

The message is written as a 32-bit length header followed by the UTF-8 bytes, one bit per color channel, skipping alpha. A photo with width × height × 3 bits of room can hold roughly that many characters divided by 8. Re-saving the PNG as a JPEG anywhere in between will destroy the hidden bits — lossy compression doesn't preserve exact pixel values.
