097 — Webcam ASCII Mirror

Turns your live camera feed into a grid of characters, redrawn every frame from the actual pixel brightness — a mirror made of text instead of a picture.

Tap "Start camera" to begin.
Uses navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia() to read the camera into a hidden <video> element, samples a downscaled frame with drawImage + getImageData on a small offscreen canvas, and maps each sampled pixel's brightness to a character from a fixed ramp before painting it back with fillText. Nothing leaves the device — the video stream is never uploaded or recorded.
