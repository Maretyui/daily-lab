091 — Speech Reader

Type or paste text, pick a voice, and hit play. The browser's built-in speech synthesizer reads it aloud while the word currently being spoken lights up below, tracked via each utterance's boundary events.

Ready.

Word timing comes from SpeechSynthesisUtterance's boundary event, which reports the character offset of each spoken word as it happens — no fixed-interval guessing. Voice list availability and boundary-event support vary by browser/OS.
