115 — WebGL Plasma

A raw WebGLRenderingContext — no Three.js, no canvas 2D — runs a single full-screen fragment shader that layers sine waves into a shifting plasma field. Dragging or tapping feeds the pointer position into the shader as a uniform, which bends the pattern around it in real time entirely on the GPU.

Drag or tap anywhere on the canvas.

The vertex shader just draws one full-screen triangle; all the actual motion happens per-pixel in the fragment shader by summing sin() waves of uTime and the distance to uPointer, then mapping that sum through cos() at three phase-shifted offsets to get an RGB color — the classic plasma-demo trick, just on a modern GPU pipeline instead of a software raster loop.
