Liquid Cursor Blob — a chain of circles chases the pointer, an SVG goo filter fuses them into one droplet

No canvas and no library: each of the six dots eases toward the one in front of it every frame (the first eases toward the real pointer), and an feGaussianBlur + high-contrast feColorMatrix merges any overlapping dots into a single soft-edged blob instead of visible separate circles.
