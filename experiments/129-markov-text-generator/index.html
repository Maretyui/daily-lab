129 — Markov Text Generator

Paste in a few paragraphs of source text. Every pair of consecutive words becomes a link in a chain; new sentences are generated by taking random walks through that chain, one real word at a time.

Click "Generate" to walk the chain.
Built from the source text split on whitespace: for every word, a map records every word that has ever followed it (a first-order Markov chain). Generation starts at a random word and repeatedly picks a random entry from the current word's list of followers, so the output is only ever made of word-pairs that actually occurred in the source — a different "random" sentence every time, built entirely from Map<string, string[]> lookups with no outside model.
