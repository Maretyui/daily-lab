042 — Color Wheel Picker

A pure CSS conic-gradient hue wheel with a radial white-to-transparent overlay for saturation/lightness. Pointer position on the disc is converted to polar coordinates, giving hue from the angle and saturation from the distance from center.

HSL: hsl(0, 100%, 75%)
HEX: #ffffff

Click or drag inside the wheel to pick a color.
