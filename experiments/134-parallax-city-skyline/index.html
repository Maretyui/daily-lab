134 — Parallax City Skyline

Four layers of procedurally generated buildings drift past at different speeds as you drag, with a sprinkle of lit windows that flicker on their own. Drag or swipe the canvas to pan; regenerate for a brand new skyline.

Drag to pan. 4 parallax layers.

Each layer is a deterministic sequence of random buildings (seeded with Math.random at generation time, not re-rolled every frame) drawn once onto an offscreen canvas wider than the viewport, then re-drawn into view shifted by panX * layer.speed — the back layer barely moves, the front layer moves almost 1:1 with your drag, which is the entire parallax illusion. A handful of windows per building toggle on a random interval via setInterval to fake life after dark.
