143 — Palette Extractor

Pick a photo and it's quantized entirely on your device — every pixel's RGB gets bucketed to the nearest step of 32, the buckets are counted, and the eight most common ones come back as hex swatches. No upload, no server round-trip, just getImageData and a frequency table.

Quantizing instead of ranking exact colors matters here — a real photo rarely repeats the same precise RGB triple twice, but it repeats regions of color constantly. Rounding each channel to the nearest 32 collapses millions of possible colors down to a few thousand buckets, and the ones with the most pixels are what a person would call the photo's dominant colors.
