095 — Haptic Rhythm Trainer

The device buzzes a steady beat through the Vibration API. Tap the circle in time with each pulse — the closer your tap lands to the actual beat, the better your score.

Avg offset (ms)
Uses navigator.vibrate() to pulse a short buzz on every beat, alongside a visual pulse for devices or browsers that don't support it. Tap timing is measured against the beat schedule with performance.now(), not against the vibration or animation firing, so results stay accurate regardless of rendering delays.
