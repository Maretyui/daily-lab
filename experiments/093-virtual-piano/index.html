093 — Virtual Piano

One octave, playable with a mouse, touch (including sliding across keys and multi-finger chords), or the computer keyboard (A–K row for white keys, W E T Y U for the black keys in between).

Tap a key, or press a key on your keyboard, to start.

Each key press schedules a plain oscillator through the Web Audio API with a short attack/release envelope. Sliding a held pointer across keys (glissando) is tracked by looking up document.elementFromPoint() under each pointermove rather than relying on individual elements' hover/enter events, so it keeps working mid-drag even once the finger has left the key it started on. Each touch gets its own pointerId, so multiple fingers can hold a chord at once.
