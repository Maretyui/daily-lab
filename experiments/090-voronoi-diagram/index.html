090 — Voronoi Diagram

Tap empty space to drop a new seed point, drag an existing one to move it. Every pixel is colored by whichever seed is nearest — a brute-force Voronoi partition recomputed on every change.

0 points
Rendered at a deliberately low internal resolution (140×140) so a nearest-seed scan over every pixel stays cheap, then scaled up with image-rendering: pixelated for the faceted look.
